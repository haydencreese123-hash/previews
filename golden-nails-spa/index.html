Our story

A calm room, and nails done the way you asked

Golden Nails & Spa is a nail bar and spa in The Quarry Shopping Center on New Brighton Blvd in Minneapolis. Classic mani-pedis through to gel, and care in between.

Tools are kept clean, stations neat, and the service calm. We listen to what you want, and we explain aftercare in plain words — which is usually the difference between a set that still looks right in two weeks and one that does not.

Reviewers name Kay and May as technicians, and one describes the owner doing her nails herself.
