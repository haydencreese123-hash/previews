Our story

Bring a picture. Leave with it on your nails.

T T Angel Nails sits in Sibley Plaza on 7th St W in St Paul. Across 273 Google reviews the same thing comes up more than anything else: someone walked in with a reference photo and walked out with it recreated. Acrylic sets, shaping, pedicures and a wide colour selection fill out the rest.
