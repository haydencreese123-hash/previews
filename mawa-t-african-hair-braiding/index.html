Our story

Braiding is the whole business

Mawa T African Hair Braiding is on University Ave W in St Paul. Across 168 Google reviews, braids are named 49 times — by a wide margin the most-mentioned thing on the listing. Box braids come up by name too, and so does braid size.

Braiding takes hours, and how the finished style looks is decided before the first section is parted: the size of the braids, the length, and how tight you want them. Say what you want at the start, and bring a picture if you have one — it is the single best way to leave with the style you pictured.
