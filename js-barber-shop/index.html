Our story

A neighbourhood shop on Bass Lake Rd

JS Barber Shop sits on Bass Lake Rd in Crystal. Across 241 Google reviews the same words keep coming back: fades, trims, and work customers describe as meticulous. The shop runs in English and Spanish — reviewers point it out often enough that it is clearly part of why people choose it. Se habla español.

Barbers here get asked for by name. One reviewer, sixteen years in Minnesota, says they found their favourite barber on the first visit.
