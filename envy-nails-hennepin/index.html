Our story

The people are the reason people come back

Envy Nails Hennepin sits on Hennepin Ave in Minneapolis. Read through 221 Google reviews and one thing outnumbers everything else: kind staff, mentioned in twenty of them — more than double the next most common thing. Knowledgeable technicians come up nine times. The salon is listed as LGBTQ+ friendly on Google.

Reviewers name Nina and Eric as their technicians, and describe the owner as nice and friendly without ever naming them. One visitor from out of town writes that the host recommended restaurants and places to visit around the neighbourhood.
